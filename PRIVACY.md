# Empty Chair: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Empty Chair is a Forge app for Atlassian Confluence and Jira, built by Philipp Schaefer. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

## Where the app runs

Empty Chair runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian"). It does not send data to any server outside Atlassian, and it has no external services, analytics, or tracking.

## What the app reads

When you open Empty Chair on a Confluence page, or ask the Empty Chair Rovo agent or MCP tools about a page, the app reads the following as you, with your permissions:

- the page's title, content, labels, comments, versions, and watchers;
- Jira issues, Jira Service Management requests, and Confluence pages that mention what the page changes;
- from the Atlassian Teamwork Graph, which people viewed the page in the last 30 days and which Atlassian teams people belong to.

Empty Chair only shows what you can already see in Confluence and Jira.

## How the app uses AI

Page content and the titles of related issues and pages are processed by Forge LLMs, Atlassian's hosted large language models, to work out what a decision changes and to draft reasons and invitations. This processing happens on Atlassian's platform under Atlassian's terms.

## What the app stores

The app stores the result of each analysis in Forge storage, inside your Atlassian site. A result contains the page title, team names, the titles of related issues and pages, and the names and Atlassian account IDs of the people suggested as contacts.

- Cached results for a page version are deleted after 6 hours.
- The latest result per page is deleted after 30 days.
- All data is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

## What the app writes

The app writes to your site only when you ask it to:

- it posts a page comment with invitations after you review and confirm them;
- the optional demo setup creates Confluence spaces and pages whose keys start with "EC".

## Sharing

Empty Chair does not sell or share data with anyone. It has no access to data outside your Atlassian site.

## Your choices

You can revoke the app's access to your account at any time under your Atlassian account settings (Connected apps). A site admin can uninstall the app, which deletes all stored data.
